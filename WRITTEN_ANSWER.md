# Script, Zapier or Flow?

Flow, mostly. I'd get the booking into Salesforce with the scheduling tool's own integration (Calendly's actually runs on Flows you can edit), or a simple Zapier step if there isn't one, and then build the actual rules in Flow. The big reason is upkeep. I'd be a team of one at first, and anyone with Salesforce admin access can open a Flow and see what it's doing. A custom script needs a developer every time a rule changes, and Zapier gets hard to follow once there's this much branching. I've also lived with a sloppy Salesforce-to-Salesloft sync and no single source of truth, so I'd rather keep the rules where the data lives. Flags would go to a review queue in Salesforce so someone actually owns them, not just a Slack message. Flow isn't great with answers like "somewhere around 8-12?", so I'd fix the form too and make provider count a dropdown split at 10. The script I wrote isn't wasted either. It's the spec, and its 11 events are the test cases for the Flow.
